Read biblical narratives within the ancient writers' framework, and avoid using a shared pattern alone to decide whether a story is true or borrowed. Moses and Sargon both have basket narratives, but their circumstances differ: Sargon's basket goes downriver, while Moses stays among the reeds under his sister's watch.
